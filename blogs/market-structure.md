# How to Identify and Trade Market Structure

Market structure forms the foundation of price action trading. Understanding how markets move, create patterns, and establish key levels can dramatically improve your trading decisions. In this guide, we'll explore practical techniques to identify and trade market structure effectively.

## What is Market Structure?

Market structure refers to the framework in which price moves. It includes:

- Trend direction and strength
- Support and resistance levels
- Higher highs and lower lows (or vice versa)
- Key swing points
- Order blocks and fair value gaps

By understanding these elements, you can determine where price is likely to go and where it might reverse.

## Identifying Higher Timeframe (HTF) Structure

Always begin your analysis from higher timeframes before moving to lower ones. Here's how to identify the overall structure:

1. **Mark major swing points** - Significant highs and lows where price reversed direction
2. **Identify trend direction** - In uptrends, look for higher highs and higher lows; in downtrends, look for lower highs and lower lows
3. **Highlight key support/resistance zones** - Areas where price has repeatedly reacted

## Market Structure Shifts

Market structure shifts (also called "breaks in structure" or "change of character") are crucial for identifying trend changes early. Here's how to spot them:

### Bearish Shift
1. Price makes a lower low (LL)
2. Price makes a lower high (LH)
3. Price breaks below the previous LL, confirming the bearish shift

### Bullish Shift
1. Price makes a higher high (HH)
2. Price makes a higher low (HL)
3. Price breaks above the previous HH, confirming the bullish shift

## Trading the Retest Strategy

One of the most reliable market structure trading strategies is the "retest strategy":

1. Identify a clear market structure break
2. Wait for price to retest the broken structure level (previous support becomes resistance or vice versa)
3. Look for a rejection candle pattern (pin bar, engulfing, etc.) at the retest level
4. Enter in the direction of the new trend with stops beyond the rejection pattern
5. Target the next significant structure level

## Order Blocks and Fair Value Gaps

Advanced market structure trading involves identifying:

- **Order blocks**: Areas where significant buying or selling occurred before a strong move
- **Fair value gaps (FVGs)**: Places where price has moved so quickly that it left an imbalance, often represented by a gap between candles

These areas often serve as magnets for price to return to in the future, creating excellent trading opportunities.

## Practical Tips for Trading Market Structure

1. **Always respect the trend**: Trade in the direction of the higher timeframe trend
2. **Be patient**: Wait for clear structure breaks and confirmations
3. **Use multiple timeframe analysis**: Confirm structure on higher timeframes before looking for entries on lower ones
4. **Combine with volume**: High volume at structure breaks adds confirmation
5. **Use proper risk management**: Place stops beyond key structure levels

## Common Market Structure Trading Mistakes

- Trying to predict structure breaks rather than reacting to them
- Ignoring higher timeframe structure
- Over-trading minor structure levels
- Not giving trades enough room to breathe
- Failing to adjust to changing market conditions
